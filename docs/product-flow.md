# Product flow

1. Customer scans a QR code attached to a business profile
2. The frontend resolves the business slug and loads the branded customer flow
3. Customer chooses a rating, service, and leaves feedback
4. ReviewMate generates a natural-language draft from the customer’s words only
5. Customer edits or copies the draft
6. Customer chooses whether to open the Google review URL
7. Business dashboard tracks scans, feedback, AI drafts, and clicks

## Compliance guardrail

ReviewMate never fabricates prices, staff names, product details, service guarantees, or customer experiences. The AI is only a writing assistant for customer-provided text.
